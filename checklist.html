If your visa is refused

This isn't a dead end. A refusal comes with a written reason from UKVI — the same day you get it, message James O. and he'll tell you within 48 hours whether it's a reapply-immediately case (most common: a documentation gap) or worth a formal appeal. Deposit refund policies vary by university; ask your application advisor before you reapply anywhere else. You are not on your own for this, and it doesn't mean starting over from zero.
