What Atlas actually stores today

This build has no live backend collecting personal data yet. What exists is demonstrated with the same account throughout ("Harman Sidhu") — nothing you'd enter into a real signup is sent anywhere. The only things this browser stores locally (never transmitted) are:

  • Whether you're signed in on this browser, and whether you've finished onboarding.
  • Your light/dark theme choice.
  • A university you've added to or removed from your shortlist.

All of it lives in this browser's own storage and clears if you clear your browser data. Nothing here is a server, a database, or a third party.

What isn't decided yet

What real personal data Atlas will collect once it has a real backend, how long it's retained, who it's shared with (including service partners), and how a student can access, correct or delete it — none of that is drafted. Treat this page as absent, not as agreed-to, until it's replaced with a real, reviewed policy.
